## Reuse observed floors during complete room layout

The original map-backed layout workflow now derives excavation costs from raw
wall/floor evidence, not caller site permissions. The complete selected mask is
recounted against its original capture, including corridors and entrances.
A new observed objective preserves the old default search and saved bytes.
`--require-optimal-layout` gates all surveyed exports without renewing budgets.
See `docs/OBSERVED_ROOM_EXCAVATION_COSTS.md`. No native or admission rule changes.
