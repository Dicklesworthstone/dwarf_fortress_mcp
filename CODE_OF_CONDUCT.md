# Code of Conduct

This project exists to make agentic control of complex simulations rigorous, inspectable, and
safe. Contributors are expected to discuss ideas and evidence rather than people; distinguish
facts, measurements, hypotheses, and preferences; disclose uncertainty; and make criticism
specific enough to be acted upon.

Harassment, threats, discrimination, doxxing, deliberate sabotage, and sustained bad-faith
behavior are not acceptable. Maintainers may edit or remove contributions and restrict
participation when necessary to preserve a productive technical community.

Report serious conduct problems privately to the repository owner. Reports will be handled as
confidentially as practical.
