remains in progress).

through `df://session/{session_id}/work`, independently of MCP Tasks. Catalog
pages link exact `action-{progress_digest}` and `obligation-{progress_digest}`
details. Details link the original typed plan archive and its retained goal. The original
plan digest, archive object digest and step identity stay distinct. Contradictory
known original custody refuses instead of being presented as missing.

They expose original deadlines, required stability, last retained monitor status
and next eligible observation tick without taking a new sample. Failed or
verified proof cannot imply physical quiescence. Recovered steps retain their
original proof identity and recovery boundary without inventing an ActionId or
restoring mutation authority. Unavailable original custody cannot authorize a
fresh physical interpretation. Shared-world original ownership is explicit.

complete retained progress; cancellation can invalidate them at an unchanged
world tick. Current whole-fortress Observe and Query authorization and durable
owner fencing precede disclosure. Complete world/original/receipt source limits,
wall deadlines and escaped output bounds apply before publication, including
small pages. Pages retain whole rows and resource replies use private zero TTL.

and durable custody. Current goal truth is explicitly not evaluated. The eleven
